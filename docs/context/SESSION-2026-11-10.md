## Product and authorization

The original storage problem led to selective native cache routing rather than moving every macOS cache. The Mac and Chrome must remain usable without external storage. The product subsequently expanded to both disk exploration and reviewed cache management, under the DiskWing name, approved Lift identity, MIT license, Apple Silicon/macOS 14+ scope and PT/EN interface. The historical approved cache plan and roadmap remain in docs/plans/.

The maintainer approved autonomous implementation, independent review, tests, branches/worktrees, PRs, merges and publication within docs/AUTONOMOUS-DELIVERY.md, without purchasing services. That scope preserves Chrome, Bun/uv originals, private archives, legacy CacheSSD identifiers/settings/digests, explicit consent and generated-data testing. It does not authorize private participant outreach or personal cache operations merely to test the app.

The maintainer later explicitly requested installation/opening of the local candidate to validate the interface, then requested that all work, context and memory be documented. Installation/launch authorization is distinct from approval of the interface or consent to move data.
